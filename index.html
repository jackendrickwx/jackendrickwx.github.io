Forecast Discussion

As of: 9:45am, Tuesday, March 10th

Today & tomorrow will be some of the warmest March days in quite a while, with temperatures in the low to mid 80s both days. Records may fall both days. Scattered rain showers with a few rumbles of thunder are expected today amidst mostly sunny conditions. Wednesday is dry, before a cold front arrives early Thursday morning and produces widespread rain... likely between 5AM and noon, but tapering off in the late morning hours.

The warmest temperature of Thursday (near 70 degrees) will occur at 12AM sharp, with decreasing values for the rest of the day. 7AM temps should be near 60 degrees before the main front arrives, and we spend the rest of the day in the 50s with gusty north-northwest winds. Southwesterly flow returns on Friday, sending milder air into the region once more, as we make a run back to the low 60s following a chilly Friday morning in the mid 30s.

Saturday will be the pick of the weekend: mostly sunny and 70s. A few more showers and clouds may be in store for Sunday ahead of another cold front that will produce a breezy and cooler Monday, with showers along the front in the morning hours.
